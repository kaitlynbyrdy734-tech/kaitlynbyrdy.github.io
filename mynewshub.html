HEAR ABOUT THE TROLLY STRIKE

NEWSBOY GETS A RALLY TOEGTHER TO FIGHT FOR BETTER PRICES

Newsboy Jack Kelly makes a promise to the newsboys that he will better the prices for newspapers! Jack says, "this strike will mean the world to us, it'll bring us a fprtune fighting for justice!". In the heart of NYC, the rally started at 3:00 and went until midnight. Many of the boys wrote banners and posters expressing their feelings toward the head of the newspapers who weren't giving their fair share. Many of the people walking by gave the boys gratitude and even pointers on how to really get what you fought for. By the end of the evening, the head walked out and proposed a truce. Expressing that the newsboys will have their fair share into getting better prices for the papers. This is a day that none of the newsboys will forget, as they won justice and respect!.
